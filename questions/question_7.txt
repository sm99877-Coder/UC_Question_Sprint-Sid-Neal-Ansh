
To determine the community college enrollment rate, we analyzed the 2023 Bay Area high school cohort from the dataset. Out of 63,578 total graduates across the region, 21,644 enrolled in a California Community College within 12 months, resulting in a regional enrollment rate of 34.0%. To ensure accuracy, we calculated this figure by pooling total enrollments across all schools before dividing by total graduates. This aggregate approach prevents smaller high schools from disproportionately weighting the regional average. As a sanity check, shifting the denominator to total high school completers yields 33.6%, while taking an unweighted average of individual school rates gives 33.5%—confirming that the actual enrollment rate consistently lands right at ~34%.
